# 03. Project Background

Healthcare institutions such as hospitals, clinics, and medical centers often face major challenges in managing patient records, appointments, prescriptions, billing, and administrative operations using traditional paper-based methods. Manual systems can result in misplaced records, duplicated data, delayed information retrieval, appointment conflicts, and increased operational costs. These issues reduce the efficiency and quality of healthcare services provided to patients.

With the rapid growth of information technology, healthcare organizations require reliable digital systems to manage medical data securely and efficiently. A computerized Medical Management System can streamline healthcare operations, reduce paperwork, improve communication between patients and medical staff, and provide faster access to healthcare information.

The proposed Medical Management System aims to provide a centralized web-based platform that automates healthcare management processes such as patient registration, appointment scheduling, doctor management, medical record maintenance, prescription handling, billing, and report generation. The system will improve operational efficiency, reduce manual errors, and enhance the overall quality of healthcare services.

# 06. Problem Statement

Many hospitals and clinics still rely on manual or semi-digital methods to manage patient records, appointments, prescriptions, billing, and healthcare administration. These traditional systems often cause issues such as data redundancy, inefficient document retrieval, appointment scheduling conflicts, excessive paperwork, human errors, and delays in healthcare services.

Healthcare staff spend significant time handling records manually, which reduces productivity and negatively impacts patient satisfaction. In addition, the absence of a centralized digital platform makes it difficult to access and manage patient information efficiently.

Therefore, there is a need for a secure and efficient Medical Management System that can automate healthcare operations, improve patient record management, streamline appointment scheduling, and support better healthcare service delivery.

---

# 07. Project Objectives

## General Objective

To develop a web-based Medical Management System that improves healthcare management through digitalization and automation.

## Specific Objectives

1. To develop a centralized system for managing patient records digitally.

2. To automate appointment booking, scheduling, cancellation, and rescheduling processes.

3. To implement secure user authentication and role-based access control for patients, doctors, and administrators.

4. To manage prescriptions, laboratory reports, and billing information efficiently.

5. To generate reports and dashboards for administrative purposes.

6. To reduce paperwork, manual errors, and operational delays in healthcare institutions.

7. To improve the accessibility and quality of healthcare services.

---

# 08. Proposed Solution / Project Description

The proposed Medical Management System is a web-based application designed to automate and improve healthcare management operations within hospitals and clinics.

The system will support multiple user roles including patients, doctors, receptionists, and administrators. Patients will be able to register accounts, book appointments, view medical histories, and access prescriptions and reports. Doctors will manage appointments, patient records, prescriptions, and medical histories. Administrators and receptionists will manage users, appointments, billing, and system reports.

The system will include:

• User registration and login system

• Role-based access control

• Patient management

• Doctor management

• Appointment scheduling system

• Electronic medical records

• Prescription management

• Laboratory report management

• Billing and payment management

• Dashboard and analytics

• Notification and reminder system

The solution will improve healthcare efficiency, reduce paperwork, and ensure secure management of sensitive medical data.
