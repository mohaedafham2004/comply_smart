01.  Project Background 

Chronic diseases such as diabetes and heart disease are among the leading causes of mortality worldwide. Early identification of individuals at risk can significantly improve treatment outcomes and reduce healthcare costs. However, healthcare professionals often need to analyze multiple patient health indicators - including glucose levels, blood pressure, cholesterol, body mass index, and age - which can be time-consuming and prone to human error. 

This project proposes an AI-powered disease risk prediction system that utilizes machine learning algorithms to analyze patient health data and estimate the likelihood of developing chronic diseases. The system aims to support healthcare providers and medical laboratories by providing early risk assessments and recommendations. Furthermore, MLOps practices will be incorporated to ensure efficient deployment, monitoring, and maintenance of the predictive model. 

 

02.  Problem Statement 

Many individuals remain unaware of their risk of developing chronic diseases until symptoms become severe. Healthcare providers and medical laboratories often rely on manual interpretation of patient health records and laboratory reports, which can be time-consuming and may delay early intervention. As the number of patients increases, analyzing large volumes of health data becomes increasingly challenging. 

There is a need for an intelligent system that can automatically evaluate patient health indicators and identify high-risk individuals at an early stage. Such a system would assist healthcare professionals in making informed decisions, improve preventive healthcare, and reduce the burden on medical institutions. 

 

03.  Project Objectives 

To collect, preprocess, and analyze healthcare datasets related to diabetes and heart disease prediction by the end of the first development phase. 

To develop and evaluate machine learning models capable of predicting disease risk with an accuracy greater than 85%. 

To design and implement a user-friendly web application that allows healthcare professionals to obtain real-time disease risk predictions. 

To deploy and monitor the machine learning model using MLOps practices, including Docker, GitHub Actions, and cloud-based deployment before project completion. 

 

04.  Proposed Solution 

The proposed solution is an AI-powered web-based healthcare decision-support system designed to predict the risk of chronic diseases using patient health data. Healthcare professionals will be able to enter patient information such as age, blood pressure, glucose levels, cholesterol levels, BMI, and other relevant medical indicators. The data will be processed and analyzed using machine learning models trained on publicly available healthcare datasets.
